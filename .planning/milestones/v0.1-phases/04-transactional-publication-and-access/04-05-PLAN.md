<objective>
Prove PUB-05 the realistic way (D-74): from a second, genuinely separate connection authenticated as the reader role with its own password, discover the published tables, run a representative spatial query that exercises the GiST index, and confirm a write is denied. This is also the identity real QGIS/GIS consumers connect as, so the proof doubles as the access contract.

Purpose: PUB-04 (reader has SELECT but no write) and PUB-05 (reader can discover, select, spatially query, and is denied writes) are proven end-to-end by a real login, not by reading grant metadata. The write-denial is security-critical: a silent grant-model regression must hard-stop, not warn.

Output: reader-verification functions in `vicmap_acquire/publish.py` returning a `ReaderVerification` result, plus `tests/test_publish.py` coverage including a negative test that a writable role trips READER_WRITE_NOT_DENIED. Live tests skip without a DSN.
</objective>
